# Accounting

The idea of this game is to play as the owner of a business interrogating the numbers. The game pretends to be about business optimization at first, but then it becomes clear that something fishy is going on and has to discover it through the accounting, and in particular by "audits" that ask for more information. Most of the game is looking at spreadsheets and numbers, but there is also email to keep things more personal and fun - and the emails are driven by what the player does. Audits take time and everything has to happen by a board meeting deadline.

## Setting

The setting should be running a regional ferry company in a fictional country's archipelago (this is inspired by Finland except that it needs to be a private company). You carry passengers and cargo. The whole game is inspired by the story of Crazy Eddie, and I want a similar story arc, but ported to this setting. This also allows it to be set in present day but feel a bit older, due to the whole seafaring business.

- The optimization layer: routes, fuel, ticket prices, maintenance.
- Suspicious numbers: vessels burning fuel while supposedly docked, cargo heavier than manifests indicate, passengers who board but never disembark.
- Audits: GPS history, weighbridge records, ticket scans, maintenance reports.
- Emails: captains, dockmasters, island officials, stranded passengers.
- Possible mystery: smuggling, and an island omitted from official route maps.

The deadline can be a board vote to shut down an unprofitable route, which will be hiding some mystery.

## Gameplay

There will be two core gameplay mechanics: making business decisions and asking for audits. Both of these will get replies with more information. (Actually, I'm not sure about the business decisions part - it wouldn't make sense to see the results in the numbers within the few weeks in which this game takes place. Might need something else that the owner could do that's in the realm of gathering information.) All of this should take place within a spreadsheet or email application.

There will be necessarily be some education about accounting, but I want to keep it simple and not explicitly educational. This demo will have just two phases: one where you get used to the language and reporting (sales, COGS, payroll, inventory, cash, profit; the notion of quarterly reports), and another that jumps ahead in the game to where the business is booming and the books have already been cooked. This is just a prototype/demo, so make some core interactions that are good and give a menu of audit options and emails, but we don't need to plan out a huge game tree.

The spreadsheet(s) should be reasonably usable (ahead of their time for the 1990s, and using proper web technologies) - the point is not to use bad software. It can also be a bit game like, like color, highlighting the current cell, explanations of each column written with dry humor, things like that. The player should not be writing formulas. When they click on a cell, the game should offer a few options, like auditing inventory, interviewing the store manager, sampling some invoices, and asking for an inventory recount. These will give different info and take different amounts of time.
