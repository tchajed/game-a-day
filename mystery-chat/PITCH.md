# Mystery Chat

Three ordinary chats are hiding something impossible. Pick up a neighbor’s scattered trail, interview a suspiciously exact candidate, or troubleshoot a laptop that refuses to behave—then paste an unseen prompt into your favorite AI and play the conversation yourself. **Mystery Chat** turns asking good follow-up questions into a five-minute detective game: stay in character, notice what does not add up, and keep pulling the thread until the whole truth comes loose.
