# SIGNAL / SEQUENCE

**Signals keep arriving. The rules keep stacking.** You are the last line of defense in a live temporal-monitoring console: scan each colored, numbered pulse, hold several promises in your head, and strike the instant one becomes impossible. Flag too early and you damage the system; hesitate until the next signal and the violation slips through. Four escalating sequences turn the cerebral language of temporal logic into a tense five-minute game of pattern recognition under pressure.
