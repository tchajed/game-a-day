# Bad Bet

This is a game about betting where the game lies to you about your chances, and you have to do your own investigation.

The setting I'm imagining is a 2D top-down casino overworld (though I think it would be cool if it weren't obviously a casino, maybe more like a carnival) where you move between games. Each game pops up as a static background image and a simple interface to place bets and see results. The games I'm thinking of are slot machines, betting on a biased coin, etc; more pure luck, no Blackjack or anything.

The catch of this whole game is that the dealers are all lying to you about your chances. I want a coin betting dealer who promises higher chances than reality, and a slot machine operator who underestimates. Allow the player to make a large range of bets, so that their decision is how much to bet. Time will pass with every bet but only by a small amount: we will give them a chance to place hundreds of bets. Your goal is to make as much money as possible in a limited time, maybe 3 days.

A mechanic I want to experiment with in this prototype is in-game advertisements. I want two fake ones and one real one that sells you a spreadsheet app (or something similar) that tracks your results and calculates averages, so you can calculate odds yourself. Let's make these signs in the game to avoid getting too meta in this game.

I was thinking of populating this game with animals: just to mix it up a little I'm imagining a kind wolf runs the slot machine and a shady and possibly tattooed rabbit (or badger? not sure what will look good) runs the coin bet. I'd like a digital art style with detail for the static images, but simple textures for the overworld for this prototype.
