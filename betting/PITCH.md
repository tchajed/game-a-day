# Bad Bet

A mysterious carnival gives you three bright days, one small purse, and several extremely confident promises. Bet against a tattooed rabbit's “even” coin or trust a kind fox who swears her old slot machine hardly ever pays—then decide whether to trust the pitch, buy the boring ledger, and gather enough evidence to risk big before the tents disappear. **Bad Bet** is a compact game of probability, persuasion, and finding out exactly how expensive a hunch can be.
