# Bad Bet

A mysterious carnival gives you one fast-fading day, one small purse, and several extremely confident promises. Bet on a tattooed rabbit's supposedly generous toss or question a kind fox about her peculiar Pair and Run card wagers—then decide whether to trust the pitch, do the arithmetic, buy the boring ledger, and gather enough evidence to risk big before the tents disappear. **Bad Bet** is a compact game of probability, persuasion, and finding out exactly how expensive a hunch can be.
