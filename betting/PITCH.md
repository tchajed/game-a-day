# Bad Bet

A mysterious carnival gives you three bright days, one small purse, and several extremely confident promises. Bet on a tattooed rabbit's supposedly generous toss or inspect the kind fox's peculiar ten-card Silver Draw—then decide whether to trust the pitch, do the arithmetic, buy the boring ledger, and gather enough evidence to risk big before the tents disappear. **Bad Bet** is a compact game of probability, persuasion, and finding out exactly how expensive a hunch can be.
