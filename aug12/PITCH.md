# Northstar Ledger

**Every number tells a story—if you ask the right question.** Open the morning mail, follow a hunch into the accounts, and watch your private journal turn other people’s replies into a case. Find unused room on a ferry, solve an honest ticketing mistake, then leap ahead to a boardroom crisis involving secret voyages and an island erased from the map. Northstar Ledger is a three-part, five-minute conversation between emails, evidence, and the thoughts you are not ready to say aloud.
