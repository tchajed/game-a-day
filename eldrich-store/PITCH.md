# The Eldrich Store

The night market was not here yesterday, but its help-wanted sign is lit. In **The Eldrich Store**, Balthazar hires you for one night as assistant manager, where returns, break coverage, displays, invoices, and closing time all require your attention. The staff are dependable mythical creatures, the merchandise has unusual terms of sale, and each practical decision shifts the till, team morale, or the veil around the premises. Keep all three intact until Balthazar completes your end-of-shift review.
