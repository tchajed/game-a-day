# The Eldrich Store

Congratulations: the demon at the night market has reviewed your application and hired you before you finished filling it out. In **The Eldrich Store**, survive one supernatural retail shift by making five perfectly ordinary management decisions about refunds, break coverage, visual merchandising, supplier invoices, and closing time—except the staff are mythical beasts, the stock is cursed, and every sensible compromise risks the till, team morale, or the veil between worlds. Balance all three, endure Balthazar’s performance review, and try not to become Employee of the Apocalypse.
