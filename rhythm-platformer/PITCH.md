# Beatbound

**A platformer where you can win if you keep the beat.** Beatbound turns three bright, toy-box platforming roads into rhythms you can see. Learn the groove with seven generous jumps, add low-flying duck cues as the pattern quickens, then face a final straightaway packed with two-beat decisions.
