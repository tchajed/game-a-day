# Beatbound

**The route is the rhythm.** Scale a neon obstacle tower as a tiny wide-eyed beat runner, snapping between ledges on a heavy procedural bassline. Every downbeat is a decision—jump the spikes or duck the incoming flyer—and every mistimed move breaks the run. Read the Just Dance-style cue lane, trust the bass, and chain all 16 moves into one perfect, pulse-pounding climb.
