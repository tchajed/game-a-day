# Beatbound

**Run right. Jump big. Then keep up.** Beatbound turns three bright, toy-box platforming roads into rhythms you can see. Learn the groove with seven generous jumps, add low-flying duck cues as the pattern quickens, then face a final straightaway packed with two-beat decisions. Tiny bouncers, musical warnings, and a chunky procedural soundtrack make every move readable—but the beat is a guide, not a leash: go off rhythm and you can still survive by reading the road yourself.
