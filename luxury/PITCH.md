# Maison Morrow

You inherit a 75-year-old maker of luxury lunchboxes that cannot pay Friday. Across eight tense quarters, say no to easy growth, protect the impossible object, and learn why attention is cheap but access is not. Every choice pays now and reveals its true cost later—while a live competitor dashboard begs you to chase millions of perfectly irrelevant units. Can you leave behind a stronger legend, or will Maison Morrow become just another lunchbox?
